## Verdict summary

- **Identity verdict: MISMATCH.** The app is running on a stale/self-contradictory picture of who this company currently is: it never surfaces the GUJENERGY rename/symbol change, and — worse — its own research narrative describes the live corporate action **backwards** (a "demerger into GSPL Transmission Limited" that the reference shows is actually an *absorption merger* of GSPC/GSPL/GSPC Energy into Gujarat Gas). This is the most serious finding in the pack: a user reading the brief would come away with the opposite mental model of what's happening to the company.
- **2 real mismatches**, both in the growth figures, and both look like genuine bugs rather than basis confusion: revenue growth MRQ YoY is sign-flipped (app +21.1% vs. reference's independently-recomputed -8.9%/-9.0%), and earnings growth MRQ YoY is understated by more than 30x (app +32.6% vs. reference's recomputed +1032–1058%). Both packs agree on the basis (mrq_yoy, same Q4 FY26 vs Q4 FY25 quarter) — this isn't explainable by consolidated-vs-standalone or TTM-vs-quarterly, so it's a strong candidate for a data-provider or growth-field bug worth root-causing (yfinance `revenue_growth`/`earnings_growth` fields, or how the sidecar reads them).
- **10 watch-tier rows**, nearly all attributable to the same root cause the reference pack independently anticipated: Gujarat Gas's still-settling merger/rename is producing wide, genuine cross-provider disagreement on market cap, trailing P/E, EPS, 52-week high, drawdown, 52-week change, and ROE. The app is reasonably transparent about several of these (its own `derived.conflicts` block catches the market-cap discrepancy, and the brief explicitly caveats the distorted trailing P/E/EPS) — it just attributes the cause to the wrong corporate action (demerger, not merger). Two more watch rows are completeness gaps: discrete latest-quarter revenue/profit figures are trivially available from the same sources the app cites but aren't surfaced, only TTM aggregates.
- **7 OK rows**: last price, forward P/E, 52-week low, dividend yield, dividend per share TTM, book value, and currency all agree closely with the reference's most current same-day sources.
- **1 fabrication flagged**: the "demerger of the transmission business into GSPL Transmission Limited" narrative (with specific filing dates) has no support in either the app's own visible tool data or the reference pack — and directly contradicts the reference's well-sourced merger narrative. Flagged loudly; not counted in the mismatch tally per instructions since it's a narrative fabrication rather than a numeric same-basis conflict.
- **Notable internal app inconsistency (not a reference mismatch):** the top-level `fundamentals.dividend_per_share_ttm` is `null` while the same field inside the research-pipeline's `structured.fundamentals` blocks is `5.82` — the two snapshots the app itself produced disagree with each other. Didn't reach the user wrong this time (the rendered value is correct), but worth a ticket.
