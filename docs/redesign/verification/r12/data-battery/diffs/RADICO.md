## Verdict summary

- **22 figures compared** (or figure-groups where the app/reference split consolidated vs. standalone or raw vs. derived).
- **ok: 14** — identity, price, market cap, P/E, EPS, 52w high/low, drawdown, 52w change, TTM DPS, no-specials, book value, currency, ISIN-absent, sector-taxonomy.
- **watch: 6** — dividend yield (cross-provider basis conflict, already self-flagged and suppressed by the app), the declared-but-unpaid ₹9 dividend amount (found the filing, didn't extract the number), latest-quarter revenue, latest-quarter net profit (no quarterly-financials source wired into this run, honestly disclosed as unverified), and ROE (yfinance data-coverage gap, correctly nulled rather than guessed).
- **mismatch: 2 — the standout finding.** Revenue growth MRQ YoY (app +19.5% vs true +15.4%) and Earnings growth MRQ YoY (app +62.1% vs true +94.6%) both claim the identical "MRQ YoY" basis as the reference's directly-computed Q4 FY26-vs-Q4 FY25 figures, yet diverge well outside tolerance — and unlike the honestly-caveated latest-quarter/dividend gaps, these two are asserted as confident, uncaveated headline numbers in the published research brief. This is the one area of this pack that looks like a genuine data-pipeline bug (most likely a stale/misaligned quarter in the yfinance growth fields) rather than an honestly-disclosed gap, and is worth tracing at the source-provider level.
- **fabrications: 0.** Every place the app lacked a verified figure (quarterly P&L, ROE, the ₹9 dividend amount, a reconciled dividend yield), it said so explicitly or returned null — it never invented a number to fill a gap.
