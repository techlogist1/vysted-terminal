Fresh-reviewer verdicts

Two blind reviewers (given only the references + app shots, no project context):

  • Round 1 (pre-iteration): "close, minor deltas, leaning different on two axes" — flagged colder-than-warm canvas (2/5), cramped composer (2/5), and monospace prose reading as "code terminal" (2/5).
  • Round 2 (post-iteration): "close, minor deltas — the bones are right." Warmth 4/5 ("the hardest thing to get right and it's right"), typography 4/5 ("no mono-on-prose sins; mono correctly fenced to numeric data"), contrast 4/5. Residual deltas: the amber accent reads warmer than the references' teal/mustard (a deliberate brand choice — the brief mandated Cursor's amber), and the chrome is denser than the airy references (the product is a finance terminal, not a single-column reader).

Honest synthesis: the app now shares the references' visual family on every fundamental — warm flat near-black, clean proportional sans for prose/chrome with mono fenced to numeric data, a disciplined 2–3-tier contrast, a single restrained accent, zero glow primitives, and a composer-led empty state with suggestion chips. It is not pixel-identical to Perplexity/Cursor, nor should it be: it is a denser, amber-branded finance terminal. The two residual reviewer deltas (accent temperature, chrome density) are a brand decision and the product's nature, not regressions.
