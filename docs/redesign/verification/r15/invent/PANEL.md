## 1. Method

**Who judged.** Two independent judges, each scoring all 59 rows without seeing the other. Judge A: the sceptical power user (strongest tier, adversarial). This rewrite replaces the single-judge verdict: every row now carries both judges' scores and the average, kills are decided by the two-judge rule, and five criterion-level disagreements of two points or more are named and resolved in section 3. The HEAD facts below were re-verified at `913235f3`; none of the files they cite changed between the first run's `ac227f43` and this HEAD (`git diff --stat` empty).

**Angles.** Judge A: adversarial on value to the real user (the serious individual investor, small-cap and India-aware) and on whether the row is still real at HEAD. Judge B: adversarial on lifecycle cost to the maintainer and on whether the row can be demoed in a populated screenshot or transcript on the day.

**Criteria.** Five 1-5 scores per row: user value, moat fit (agent-first, local-first, BYOK, witnessed answers), feasibility inside the existing architecture and boundaries, demo quality, lifecycle. The panel score is the arithmetic mean of the two judges. Kill rule: killed by both is dead; killed by one was re-read against the backlog row and the objection and decided with a written rationale (section 7). A disagreement of two points or more on any criterion is named and resolved in section 3.

**Ranking rule.** Survivors are ordered by the value the real user gets per build-day: `avg user_value x (avg feasibility / 5) / days`, where `days` is the backlog estimate except where a judge's objection states a smaller residual verified at HEAD (BL-03: 3 days, the position half only; BL-15: 4 days as the exchange_financials reframe). Rows within 0.1 of each other are a tie broken by lifecycle, then moat fit.

**Boundaries applied.** Trading is out permanently (no broker connectivity, order placement/proposal/review, paper brokerage, live/paper switch); the user's own tracked portfolio with manual holdings, cost bases, P&L on real prices, notes and watchlists stays and the agent may reason over it. Locked: two-tier research, the R9 design system ('on' or 'on, extends it' with one primitive), the dev keystore, the plugin system, no return of the retired broker plugin. Tier-4 files out of reach for the one small build: `types/plugin.ts`, `.github/`, `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`, `LICENSE*`, the section 6.5 safety files, `CLAUDE.md`. Local-first, BYOK and the keyless default (llama3.1:8b via local ollama, keyless data sources) keep working; no new heavy dependency; nothing that needs a funded model lane, a paid data source or a GUI-only proof.
